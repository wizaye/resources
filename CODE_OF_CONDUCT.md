# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the resource. Welcome contributors regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting maintainers' decisions, contributor time, and project scope.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or sustained bad-faith interaction.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard. Consequences may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on [@wizaye's GitHub profile](https://github.com/wizaye). Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept as confidential as reasonably possible. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern is prohibited.

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing this project.
